QUESTIONS

Before you get started

Can doctors customize prescription print formats?

Yes. You can upload your clinic logo, add doctor registration numbers, customize letterheads, and choose what fields (vitals, diagnosis, notes) appear on the printed slip.

Does the queue system work on mobile and tablets?

Yes. The portal is completely responsive. Doctors and receptionists can manage the queue seamlessly from iPads, Android tablets, laptops, or smartphones.

Can we use our own custom domain?

Yes. On the Professional and Enterprise plans, your patient-facing booking site and staff portals can run on your own domain (www.yourclinic.com).

How does patient data security and privacy work?

Patient records are encrypted, access is strictly governed by role-based authentication, and staff sessions can be revoked instantly by the administrator.

Can we integrate our local pharmacy or laboratory?

Yes. The system includes a dedicated pharmacy dispensing portal that receives real-time electronic prescriptions the moment a doctor completes a consultation.

What happens if our internet drops mid-consultation?

Session protection retains your active draft notes and prescriptions in the browser workspace so nothing typed during a consultation is lost if connectivity momentarily dips.

We already have patient records elsewhere — can you migrate them?

Yes. Send an export from your current system and our team maps it into Clinixa as part of onboarding, at no extra charge on Professional and Enterprise plans.

Who pays for the WhatsApp messages — you or us?

Clinixa builds and manages the integration, but the messages themselves run on your clinic's own WhatsApp Business API account with Meta. Meta bills your clinic directly, per conversation, at their published rates — we don't mark that up or route it through our billing.

What about online payments — do you charge extra for a payment gateway?

We can integrate a payment gateway of your choice (Razorpay, PayU, Stripe, etc.) so patients can pay online at booking. The gateway's own transaction charges are billed by that provider directly to your clinic, at their standard rates — we don't add a markup on top.
